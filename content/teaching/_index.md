My teaching connects mechanics, numerical analysis, and scientific computing.

## Featured course and open book

### ME/BME 736 — Advanced Finite Element Methods

In Fall 2026, I am teaching a graduate course on the analysis and implementation
of advanced finite element methods. The course uses heat conduction and solid
mechanics as recurring model problems and FEniCSx for computational work. Topics
include error estimation, adaptivity, constrained and mixed methods, nonlinear
and transient formulations, and PDE-constrained optimization.

[Explore the open course and book](https://ceadpx.github.io/finite-element-methods/)
